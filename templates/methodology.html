Methodology

Every definition used in this report, written so a sales or marketing manager can follow it. Each one links to the SQL that computes it.

Data

Seven CSV exports are loaded into DuckDB, an analytical SQL database that runs inside the Python process (no server). Six are shaped like HubSpot exports (owners, companies, contacts, deals, deal stage history, marketing touchpoints) and one like a Stripe-style billing export (subscriptions). The audit date is fixed at {{ settings.report.as_of }} so results are reproducible.

Reconciliation categories

CategoryMeaning
MatchedBoth sides agree within ${{ "%.2f" | format(settings.recon.tolerance_usd) }} after normalization.
CRM-onlyClosed-won in the CRM but no active subscription: never invoiced, billed elsewhere, or churned without a CRM update.
Billing-onlyActive subscription with no closed-won deal: a self-serve signup or a sale never logged in the CRM.
Amount mismatchSame currency, but the annual values differ: a discount, extra storefront, price change, or a deal with no amount.
Term mismatchOne side is exactly 12x the other: a monthly price entered as an annual value, or the reverse.
Currency mismatchThe CRM and billing record the account in different currencies.

What this demo doesn't do

It reads CSV files, not a live CRM. It doesn't write anything back: fixes are exported for a person to import. ARR here is contracted recurring value, not revenue recognized by finance.
