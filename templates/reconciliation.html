How the join works

The CRM and billing don't share an ID, so accounts are joined on normalized domain (https://www.Shop.example/ becomes shop.example). That's a compromise: two brands under one parent can share a domain, and a customer can be billed under a different one. In production you'd sync the CRM company ID into billing so the match is exact. Categories are checked in this order: Billing-only, CRM-only, Currency mismatch, Matched (within ${{ "%.2f" | format(settings.recon.tolerance_usd) }}), Term mismatch (one side exactly 12x the other), Amount mismatch.
