Why merges are never automatic

A merge in HubSpot combines two records' history and can't be cleanly undone. Two records with the same email domain can be two different brands under one parent company, and two contacts can share a team inbox. A wrong merge loses data and attaches deals to the wrong customer, so the tool only suggests pairs and a person decides.
