Reading this

Before normalization, "Webinar", "Newsletter" and "google.com" each look like their own channel, so the real channels are undercounted. After mapping aliases with config/utm_governance.yml, each channel is counted once. Multi-touch models (linear, time-decay, W-shaped) would split credit across every touch instead of giving it all to one; this demo keeps the two simplest models so the effect of clean UTMs is easy to see.
